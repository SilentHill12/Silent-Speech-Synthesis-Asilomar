Open-vocabulary results, voiced EMG

Harvard sentences reconstructed from voiced EMG. Word error rate is shown per sentence — the last two are failures we haven’t fixed yet.

These are voiced-EMG reconstructions, not silent synthesis. The main demo is silent EMG on a closed vocabulary. Open-vocabulary silent synthesis is what we’re working toward — we’re not there yet.

Every clip below comes from a recording session the model never saw. The badges say whether the sentence was also new: “unseen sentence” means the text never appeared in training at all, while “seen sentence” means the text was in training but this recording of it was not.
